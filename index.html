About Us

Majilpur Athletic Club is dedicated to fostering a love for sports among the community, developing champions through world-class coaching and facilities.

Championship Legacy

Decades of regional and district titles across cricket and football.

Community Driven

Building stronger bonds through sport, outreach, and youth development.

Expert Coaching

Certified coaches with district and state-level training experience.

Special programmes for juniors to discover and develop their potential.
